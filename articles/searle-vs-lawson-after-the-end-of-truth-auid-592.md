# Searle vs Lawson: After the End of Truth

*HEAD TO HEAD: is independent reality a fantasy?*

**By Head to Head** | *Issue 39, 27th October 2015* | *Read time: approx. 1 mins* | *113 words*

![Searle vs Lawson: After the End of Truth](https://cdn.iai.tv/assets/Uploads/_resampled/FillWzEyODAsNzIwXQ/Searle-39-2.webp)

---

A head to head from two combatants in the recently released IAI tv debate ‘[After the End of Truth](https://iai.tv/video/after-the-end-of-truth)’ which has caused something of a storm. The debate was not short of disagreement. On one side one of the world’s most influential analytic philosophers, John Searle, was defending realism and philosophy’s classical values: [objectivity and truth](http://iainews.iai.tv/articles/objectivity-and-truth-auid-548). On the other philosopher and director of the IAI, Hilary Lawson, argued that realism had failed and an [independent reality was a fantasy](http://iainews.iai.tv/articles/after-the-end-of-truth-part-2-auid-552/). So we’ve given both of them a right to reply.
